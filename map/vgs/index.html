VGS

35 claims (21 company site, 12 announced, 2 reported) · latest Nov 6, 2026 · updated November 12, 2026

VGS sits in the payment step of an agent purchase, acting as a processor-agnostic token service that stores payment credentials so agents and merchants can transact securely2423,2413. It also offers merchant tools such as an Agentic Commerce Hub and Agentic Autofill for checkout2426,2432. It relies on the card networks, with direct network token integrations and partnerships with Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay, and American Express ACE2361,2419,2420,2421. Most recently, Gondola, nekuda, and PayOS announced they use or integrate VGS2424,2435,2437.

Core products

What VGS sells, in its own words, from its product pages.

  • 3DS: Orchestrate 3D Secure authentication across payment processors with vendor independence.source →
  • Account Updater: Updates card-on-file information continuously or in real-time to reduce declined transactionssource →
  • Account Validation: Verify cards are valid and in good standing to prevent fraud and chargebacks.source →
  • Agentic API Pass-Through: Allow agents to complete transactions safely with tokenized or raw payment data.source →
  • Agentic Autofill: Autofill tokenized card data into checkout processes for agents.source →
  • Agentic Commerce Hub: Enable AI agents to complete secure, compliant payment transactions with merchants.source →
  • Agentic Commerce Hub for Merchants: Provides infrastructure for merchants to enable payments and data transparency in agentic commercesource →
  • Agentic Network Tokens: Authenticate and future-proof agent-driven payment transactions.source →
  • AI Data Firewall: Protect sensitive data in AI workflows by automatically detecting and tokenizing payment data, PII, and other sensitive fields.source →
  • Card Attributes: Retrieve detailed card data beyond BIN lookups for payment routing and fraud detection.source →
  • Card Management Platform: Store, enrich, and manage payment data through a single API integration.source →
  • Collect: Create compliant forms that securely capture payment and personal informationsource →
  • Network Tokens: Substitute card numbers with processor-agnostic tokens to increase authorization rates and reduce fraudsource →
  • Tokenized Account Numbers: Replace bank account numbers with secure tokens for ACH payments and payroll.source →
  • Vault: Store, protect, and tokenize sensitive data in a PCI-compliant vault.source →
  • Wallet Payments: Centrally manage digital wallet payments across multiple payment service providers.source →
