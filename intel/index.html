Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Monday, November 16, 2026

OpenAI's launch of ChatGPT Wallet marks a pivotal moment in agentic commerce infrastructure: payment networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are deploying tokenized credentials to enable AI agents to transact while keeping real card details hidden from merchants and agents. Meanwhile, Amazon's expansion of Alexa for Shopping to 13 markets demonstrates parallel momentum in agent-driven commerce at scale. The emerging consensus, from Nuvei's advisory and Google-Nvidia bank partnerships, is that merchants and financial institutions must actively architect how agents interact with their systems, moving beyond passive acceptance to deliberate agent authorization and control frameworks.
