Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Sunday, November 22, 2026

Visa's Agentic Commerce Benchmark provides the first rigorous public assessment of how AI models perform when autonomously shopping, revealing that failure modes in agent purchasing, where the model proceeds despite choosing wrong items, occur in nearly half of all runs. This signals growing market attention to the operational and dispute risks of machine-to-machine payments, a core infrastructure question as agentic commerce scales. Meanwhile, stablecoin infrastructure continues to emerge as the connective tissue for payments interoperability, with approaches treating blockchain and stablecoins as the ledger beneath all payment networks.
