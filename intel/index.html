Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Tuesday, November 24, 2026

Agentic commerce infrastructure accelerated this week across payments, identity, and merchant tooling. Visa expanded Agentic Ready to 140+ participants while PayPal, Klarna, and AWS launched or refined agent payment capabilities; concurrently, fraud detection and agent access control emerged as critical gaps, with Sumsub finding only 12% of businesses can revoke agent access. Regulatory and competitive positioning shifted notably: PayPal differentiated liability via virtual cards, Citi shifted risk to consumers using third-party agents, and eBay announced plans to productize shipping and authentication as platform services, signaling that capital-intensive, physical services may be the next defensible moat against AI disruption in commerce.
