Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Saturday, October 31, 2026

Today's signals reveal three converging themes in John's scope. First, agentic commerce is reshaping payments: Plaid's Brian Dammeir outlined how AI agents will optimize payment methods differently than humans, and Shopify's vault signals enable trusted payments without CVV friction. Second, fraud detection is democratizing via decision models (Jev, Duly) and AI agents (Casap), lowering the bar for real-time ML scoring and shifting the build-vs-buy calculus. Third, bot defense and agent access control are becoming platform infrastructure: Cloudflare GA'd private origins with bot management for internal APIs and MCP servers, and OpenAI signaled robots.txt standardization for AI training—both core Know Your Agent (KYA) infrastructure plays.
