Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Agentic commerce matured significantly this week with major platforms, Google, Amazon, and Zalando, launching agent-powered checkout and shopping workflows. However, Visa's benchmark exposed critical reliability gaps: 42.8% of AI shopping agents failed test scenarios, with over half of failures still generating purchase recommendations despite unmet customer requirements. Meanwhile, infrastructure for agent identity (Descope, Sardine's Know Your Human protocol) and agent-authorized payments (IXOPAY, Pliant's $80M raise, Tempo Credit, Mercury's agent bank access, and PNC's stablecoin pilots) reached critical scale, alongside stablecoin infrastructure (BVNK's Open USD, Raycash) enabling machine-to-machine settlement. The week underscores a widening gap between technical capability and production-grade reliability for autonomous transactions.

Funding

  • OpenAI: Reportedly raised $11 billion at $1.25 trillion valuation; round could reach $20 billion before closing, falling short of $30 billion target amid investor uncertainty on AI valuations. TLDR AI

Launches

  • Google: Expanded agent-powered checkout to Canada; shoppers can purchase from participating retailers via Google Search AI Mode and Gemini using Universal Commerce Protocol and Google Pay. Agentic Commerce Frontier by M FARAH →
  • BVNK: Made Open USD live for business customers; eligible businesses outside EEA can mint and redeem Open USD stablecoin at zero cost on Ethereum, Solana, Base and Tempo. Marcel
  • Anthropic: Launched Claude Personal Finance for paid US subscribers; connects bank accounts via Plaid to track spending, review subscriptions, and ask questions about money with no ads or training on conversations. TLDR Fintech
  • Mercury: Enabled AI agents to work inside business bank accounts under defined limits; agents can execute payments and perform banking actions within governance constraints. TLDR Fintech
  • Remote: Launched virtual expense cards built into EOR platform; employees can pay for work purchases without advancing personal funds; spending controls connect to employment data. TLDR Fintech
  • Tempo: Launched Tempo Credit connecting businesses with outside lenders; draws and repayments settle in stablecoins in under one second, 24/7; first live case with El Dorado users borrowing USDT from Tala. TLDR Crypto
  • Sardine: Implemented Know Your Human protocol enabling merchants to distinguish legitimate AI agents from unauthorized bots; passes agent parameters including device identifiers and IP to unblock agent transactions. TLDR Crypto
  • Rillet: AI accounting firm valued at $1B following $100M Series C led by ICONIQ; opened London office and serves nearly 1,000 customers across 75 countries with agentic enterprise resource planning. Marcel
  • TikTok: Launched standalone TikTok Shop app in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and Philippines; offers personalized recommendations, livestream shopping, and order tracking without main TikTok app. Shopifreaks
  • OpenRouter: Launched next-generation Auto Router and new Auto Code router specialized for coding; routes outperform old router by up to 4x by weighing eleven factors plus live pricing and provider health. TLDR AI

Partnerships

  • PNC: Piloting incoming stablecoin payments with Coinbase; eligible corporate and institutional clients can receive USDC on Ethereum, converted 1:1 to dollars in PNC deposit account. Marcel
  • Plaid: Enabled Anthropic Claude Personal Finance to connect consumer bank accounts for AI-driven financial insights and spending analysis. TLDR Fintech
  • Amazon: Replaced Prime's free Grubhub+ membership with Uber Eats benefit; US members can order restaurant delivery in Amazon Shopping app with no delivery fee on $25+ orders. Shopifreaks

Regulation

  • Coinbase: Received final OCC approval for Coinbase National Trust Company; will provide institutional custody and cannot take retail deposits or lend; plans to migrate custody business over three years. Marcel

Also notable

  • Google: Building constrained credentials in Google Wallet allowing third-party agents to pay without exposing private data; infrastructure exists with Gemini and Google Pay; focus now on product experience. TLDR Fintech
