Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Thursday, November 19, 2026

Major infrastructure providers are racing to enable agentic commerce. Payhawk's $280M raise at $2B valuation signals strong investor confidence in AI agents as a core fintech capability, with 77% of its customer base already using them daily. Simultaneously, payment networks (Visa, Klarna, Lightspark) are launching agent-ready transaction layers, while identity and access control vendors like Teleport are addressing the security challenge of autonomous agents in production. These parallel moves, from spend management to payments to access control, suggest agentic commerce infrastructure is shifting from experimental to buildable.

Funding

  • Payhawk: Raised $280M at $2B valuation in Series C led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives; 77% of customers use AI agents daily. Marcel

Launches

  • Lightspark: Launched Agent Accounts allowing AI agents to hold, send, receive money, and pay at Visa merchants with single-purchase cards or stablecoin settlements. Marcel
  • Teleport: Launched Teleport Beams GA, a trusted runtime for securing AI agents in production with delegated identity, access controls, and kill switches. Teleport
  • Klarna: Introduced AI 'concierge' assistant for shopping, reservations, email drafting, and payments via text/WhatsApp/iMessage; 77% of users engage AI agents daily. Payments Dive (Technology)
  • Plaid: Connected Instinct AI assistant to users' bank accounts via read-only financial connectivity for personalized financial conversations. Marcel

Partnerships

  • Mastercard: Expanded collaboration with Temu on digital payments and local seller support. Mastercard Newsroom

Also notable

  • Visa: Extends Agentic Ready programme to merchants, acquirers, and payment service providers with 140+ participants testing agent-initiated transactions. Marcel
