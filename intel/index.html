Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Monday, November 23, 2026

Agentic commerce is maturing rapidly as infrastructure vendors integrate payment choice frameworks and agent verification. Klarna and Adyen's open-payment-handler approach ensures AI agents can present the same payment options humans see at checkout, while Sardine's Know Your Human protocol addresses merchant concerns about agent verification. Affirm and Consumer Reports' payment-choice research shows that well-designed instructions can lift agent decision quality from 48% to 72%, establishing a usable benchmark for the emerging category.
