Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Major fintech and AI infrastructure developments this week centered on agent payments and identity. Alta's $80M funding and Airwallex integration signal institutional momentum for blockchain-based agent settlement rails, while zerohash's acquisition of agent-identity infrastructure addresses the foundational Know Your Agent (KYA) problem, linking every agent credential to verified persons/companies with granular permission controls. Regulatory progress for stablecoin and crypto-asset services (DriveWealth MiCA authorization, Axiology investment firm status) continues to build rails, and OpenAI's ChatGPT Business Hub launch demonstrates how AI agents are reshaping e-commerce discovery and checkout. Separately, co-browsing emerges as the dominant agentic commerce pattern, blurring the line between human and bot activity and challenging traditional bot defense policies.

Funding

  • Alta: Alta raised $80M Series A (Index Ventures led) for blockchain payment infrastructure enabling banks and AI agents to move money instantly with privacy; Airwallex is first partner connecting its global payments network via stablecoins across 200 countries. Marcel

Launches

  • OpenAI: OpenAI Business Hub beta lets eligible retailers claim verified business profiles in ChatGPT, update store details, sync product inventory with prices/stock, and publish a Business Agent for branded AI chat; Shopify integration simplifies verification. MarketMaze →
  • Bilt: Bilt launched Neighborhood OS, expanding beyond housing to local commerce by connecting merchants' payment, reservation, and loyalty systems; partners include Adyen, Shift4, Clover, Verifone; 8M+ members, 50K+ merchant partners. Marcel
  • MiPlata: MiPlata launched Visa prepaid card (issued by Lead Bank, built on Stripe Issuing) enabling direct USDC spending; targets underserved Latino customers in US and 16 Latin American countries. Marcel
  • Wise: Wise launched Everyday Account in Australia (PayID, BPAY, BSB details, fee-free domestic transfers); Australian customer balances reached A$2.8B by September, up 53% YoY. Marcel

Regulation

  • DriveWealth: DriveWealth (UAB DriveWealth Europe) received MiCA authorization from Bank of Lithuania; can now offer crypto-asset services across EU alongside existing MiFID II licence, enabling one regulated entity for both securities and crypto; services launching in 2027. Marcel
  • Axiology: Axiology received expanded investment firm authorization from Bank of Lithuania, enabling order reception, transmission, and execution; simplifies institutional access to tokenized money market funds; already operates DLT trading/settlement under EU DLT Pilot Regime. Marcel

Also notable

  • zerohash: zerohash acquired an agent-identity infrastructure stack and IP portfolio from former NASA AI researcher Mark Meadows (now Head of Applied AI); links every agent credential to a verified person/company controlling agent permissions, amounts, and scope. Marcel
  • Plaid: Plaid released report showing generative AI is weakening traditional fraud defenses; argues next-generation fraud prevention must analyze financial behavior across devices, accounts, and institutions; Plaid Protect built on connection patterns across 1B devices and 12K+ financial institutions. Marcel
  • Incognia: Incognia secured US patent (No. 12,764,491, granted Oct 13) for location-based user verification technology using device sensor data to validate user information. Marcel
  • Glia: Glia surpassed $100M annual recurring revenue; AI sales grew 93% YoY in Q3; deployed at 700 financial institutions reaching ~95M US consumers. Marcel
