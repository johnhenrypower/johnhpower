Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Friday, October 30, 2026

Agentic commerce infrastructure is rapidly solidifying: Shopify embeds card-network payment tokens for AI agents, Visa/Mastercard/Amex deliver passkey and token tech, and stablecoin adoption accelerates (56% of Americans see value; 77% Gen Z support). Kalshi's $40B valuation signals prediction markets entering mainstream, though Polymarket's breach underscores security risks. Cloudflare's Bot Management improvements sharpen agent-identity signal fidelity—critical for distinguishing legitimate agents from scrapers.

Launches

  • Shopify: Shopify integrates Visa, Mastercard, and American Express agentic payment tokens into Shop Pay, allowing authorized AI agents to use cards without seeing underlying credentials. PYMNTS Agentic Commerce →
  • Shopify: Shopify adds agentic payment technology to Shop Pay checkout, enabling AI shopping agents to make secure payments using card-network tokens while retaining payment protections and rewards. Marcel
  • Checkout.com: Checkout.com introduces Visa Payment Passkey for merchants globally, enabling passwordless transaction authentication. Marcel
