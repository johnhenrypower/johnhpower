Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Tuesday, November 17, 2026

The week saw major momentum in agentic commerce infrastructure: OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet went live with Amex, Mastercard, and Visa, while Mastercard piloted AI travel booking and Mambu integrated cross-border payments into core banking platforms. Concurrently, bot access control became increasingly visible, Cloudflare reported 50+ LLM penetration testing frameworks scanning customers daily, while Google and competitors enhanced invalid traffic detection. These parallel developments underscore a market inflection where agents need both frictionless payment rails and robust access governance.

Funding

  • Agentcard: Agentcard announced $8 million seed round to expand payment credentials, purchase approvals and shopping tools for agents. Agentic Commerce Frontier by M FARAH

Launches

  • OpenAI: OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, enabling AI agents to make purchases using tokenized card credentials without accessing actual card details, with per-transaction biometric approval. Agentic Commerce Frontier by M FARAH →
  • Sierra: Sierra released Personal Agent Protocol 1.0, with Shopify publishing discovery files for every store and Meta's Muse checking for protocol support. Agentic Commerce Frontier by M FARAH
  • Mastercard: Mastercard launched Mastercard Accept, enabling debit and prepaid cardholders to receive payments and access everyday acceptance capabilities through existing banking channels. Mastercard Newsroom
  • OpenAI: OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, allowing merchants to claim profiles, provide product information, offer Business Agents, and track engagement and analytics. Shopifreaks
  • BigCommerce: BigCommerce rolled out an AI shopping assistant to eligible retailers, enabling conversational commerce with product recommendations and direct cart integration. MarketMaze →
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, a cloud-hosted browser built specifically for AI agents to interact with websites, now open source under Apache 2.0 license. PYMNTS Agentic Commerce →
  • Tempo: Tempo deploying Configurable Accounts (T14) to mainnet on December 1, introducing post-quantum signatures and native multisig as default primitives for stablecoin and agent payments. TLDR Crypto

Partnerships

  • Mambu: Mambu brought Mastercard Move to banks across Asia Pacific, integrating cross-border payment capabilities directly into the core banking platform for SMEs and retail customers. Marcel
  • Booked AI: Mastercard partnered with Booked AI and Travelport to enable AI agents to search, reserve, book and purchase airline tickets while protecting customer payment information with tokenization. Payments Dive (Technology) →

Also notable

  • United Airlines: United Airlines tightened restrictions on unauthorized AI agents, citing account security, unintended transactions and regulatory requirements. Agentic Commerce Frontier by M FARAH
  • Glance: Glance launching brand-agent platform at Glance Forward event in New York, enabling emotional and personal shopping categories with image-first agent design and partnerships with PayPal and Mastercard for checkout. Retailgentic →
  • Revolut: Starling founder Anne Boden plans Lockwood, a bank for AI agents, targeting e-money license in 2027 and launch in 2028 with fee-free accounts and spending limit controls for agents. Marcel
  • Google: Google introducing Invalid Traffic Overview Card in Google Ads for campaign transparency, helping advertisers identify and avoid invalid bot traffic. Google Ads & Commerce Blog
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare documented surge in LLM-based penetration testing frameworks, with 50+ families scanning customers in recent weeks; recommends Modern Security Model with bot defense as key pillar. Cloudflare →
