Agentic Commerce Market Intel

Monday, November 9, 2026

This week's signals highlight accelerating adoption of agentic commerce infrastructure alongside regulatory clarity and significant fintech capital deployment. Visa's agentic commerce benchmark revealed a 57.2% pass rate on shopping tasks before checkout, underscoring both technical progress and real obstacles to consumer adoption; Sardine's Know Your Human solution addresses the identity and authentication gap between agents and merchants. Revolut's $115B valuation and $1B Latin America commitment reflect investor confidence in scaled fintech banking, while the OCC's new AI supervision framework provides regulatory clarity for banks deploying agents and AI systems, emphasizing control architecture (human-in/on-the-loop) and consequences over technical sophistication. Cloudflare's expanded Agents SDK for durable, persistent agent execution on its platform signals infrastructure maturation for production agentic workloads.

Funding

  • Revolut: Revolut closed a secondary share sale at $115 billion valuation, with existing shares sold to funds led by Glade Brook, Coatue, and others; now valued higher than BNY Mellon and Barclays. Marcel

Launches

  • Sardine: Sardine launched Know Your Human (KYH), a solution connecting AI agents to the real people behind them by sharing device identifier and IP address, enabling merchants to approve agent transactions with confidence. Fintech Brainfood by Simon Taylor →
  • Visa: Visa launched the Visa Agentic Commerce Benchmark testing 22 AI models from 12 labs on 15 shopping tasks; found 57.2% pass rate before checkout, with best model at 82.2% and worst at 28.9%, highlighting adoption barriers. Fintech Brainfood by Simon Taylor →
  • Amazon: Amazon began letting invited U.S. shoppers text Alexa Shopping to request products and approve purchases in conversation, with sponsored product ads appearing in replies at no initial cost to advertisers. MarketMaze →
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare expanded its Agents framework to support running Codex, Exo, and Nanocode agent harnesses on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects with persistent session history and recovery after interruptions. Cloudflare Agents Changelog →

Partnerships

  • Revolut: Revolut committed at least $1 billion to Latin America expansion over several years, with Brazil as primary focus; Mexican bank reached 1M customers in 8 months, Colombia has full banking license, and Argentina is converting Banco Cetelem to Revolut Bank Argentina. Marcel

Regulation

  • OCC (Office of the Comptroller of the Currency): OCC published a supervision framework for bank examiners to evaluate 'super intelligence' (AI) use, focusing on purpose, risk, control, oversight, resilience, and assurance; explicitly addresses external agents interacting with bank systems and stress/systemic effects. Fintech Business Weekly →
